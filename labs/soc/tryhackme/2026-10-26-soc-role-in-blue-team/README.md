## Learning objective

Understand how defensive security responsibilities fit together and how a junior analyst can communicate findings and escalate an investigation effectively.

## Concepts I can now explain

- Defensive work involves monitoring, investigation, response and improvements to security controls.
- Clear responsibilities and escalation procedures help a team coordinate its work. The exact structure varies between organisations.
- Evidence from an initial review should be documented so another investigator can understand the context and continue the analysis.
- Specialist knowledge can support a wider investigation; effective communication matters alongside technical skills.

## Interview explanation

A junior analyst needs to gather relevant context, document observations and communicate uncertainty clearly. An escalation should explain what is known, what remains unverified and why further investigation is needed. Team responsibilities differ between organisations, so I would follow the local escalation process and ask for appropriate support.

## Next practice step

Use original sample logs in a Wazuh or Splunk lab to practise alert triage without a step-by-step guide. Write an investigation note with evidence, a reasoned conclusion and an escalation recommendation. This is a planned exercise, not completed work.
